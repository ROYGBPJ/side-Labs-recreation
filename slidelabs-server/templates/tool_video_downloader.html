How to download a video

  1. Paste a direct link to a video or audio file (for example one ending in .mp4).
  2. Confirm you have the right to download it and press Fetch.
  3. The file is saved by your browser.

Good to know

This offline edition runs entirely in your browser, so it can only download files from sites that allow it. Platforms such as TikTok, YouTube and Instagram can't be downloaded without the server edition.

How to download a video

  1. Copy the video link from the site or app.
